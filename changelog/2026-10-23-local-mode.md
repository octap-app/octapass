---
title: "Planned: use Octa Pass without an account"
date: 2026-10-23
summary: Local mode lets you use Octa Pass on one device with no account and no server, with encrypted backups and one-tap sync later. It arrives in phase 2 with the browser extension.
tags: [decision, docs]
---

Octa Pass will have a **local mode**: choose **Use on this device only** at first launch, and your vault stays on that device with nothing sent anywhere.

- Autofill, suggested passwords, one-time codes, passkeys, the health check and device unlock all work offline.
- Encrypted backup files protect against losing the device, with reminders and optional automatic backups to a folder you choose.
- The vault is tied to the device's security chip where there is one, and needs a strong master password.
- **Turn on sync** later creates a free account and uploads the vault without re-entering anything.

It ships with the browser extension in phase 2, then in the desktop and mobile apps. See [use without an account](/docs/features/local-vault/) and plans 01, 03, 05 and 09.
