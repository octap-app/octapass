The Octa Pass browser extension is out as part of the public alpha:

- **[Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/octa-pass/jainmncaocgdhknhcjkdbbbmahllbjol)** for Chrome, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi and Arc. Edge installs from it too, once you allow extensions from other stores; an Edge Add-ons listing comes later.
- **[Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/octa-pass/)** for Firefox 140 and later, and Firefox for Android 142 and later.

It signs in to the hosted service at [vault.octap.app](https://vault.octap.app) by default, or to [your own server](/docs/self-hosting/connect-apps/).

- The landing page's Chrome and Firefox entries now say **Get it** and go to the listings.
- **Get the extension** in the desktop app and the Mac app opens the store for your browser instead of the docs page.
- The [browser extension](/docs/platforms/browser-extension/) docs, the [quick start](/docs/getting-started/quick-start/), the [support matrix](/docs/platforms/support-matrix/) and the FAQ link to both listings, in English and Arabic. They also give Firefox's real minimum, 140, where they said 130.

Nothing has had its external security audit yet, so keep a backup of anything important.
