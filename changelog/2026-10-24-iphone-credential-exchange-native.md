---
title: The native iPhone app moves items to and from other password managers
date: 2026-10-24
summary: On iOS 26, the native iPhone app imports from and exports to other password managers through Credential Exchange, with the same review as Android.
tags: [mobile, docs]
---

The native iPhone app now has Credential Exchange (iOS 26), as the current app does:

- **Import:** in the other app (for example Passwords), choose **Export Data to Another App**, then **Octa Pass**. After you unlock, Octa Pass shows what was sent and saves nothing until you tap **Import**.
- **Export:** **Settings → Other apps → Export to another app**, after your master password.

See [Mobile apps](/docs/platforms/mobile/#moving-between-password-managers-on-the-iphone).
