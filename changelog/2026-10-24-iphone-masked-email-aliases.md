---
title: The native iPhone app lists your masked email aliases
date: 2026-10-24
summary: Masked email in the native iPhone app shows the aliases your logins use, as the web vault does.
tags: [mobile]
---

**Masked email** in the native iPhone app now has **Aliases in use**, as in the web vault: each alias with its service, how many logins use it, and a link to each of those logins.
