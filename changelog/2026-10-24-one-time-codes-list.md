title: All your one-time codes in one list
date: 2026-10-24
summary: A new One-time codes category, next to Passkeys, lists every item with a code and shows the current code with its countdown and a copy button.
tags: [web, extension, desktop, mobile]
---

If Octa Pass is your authenticator app, the codes were spread across your logins. Now **One-time codes** in the sidebar's Categories, next to Passkeys, lists every item that has a code, with a count like the other categories: logins with a code, and codes saved on their own.

Each row shows the current code and a ring counting down to the next one. Click or tap the code to copy it (the clipboard clears after 30 seconds), or the rest of the row to open the item.

In the browser extension it's the new **Codes** tab. It works the same in the web vault and the desktop and phone apps, in English and Arabic. See [TOTP codes](/docs/features/totp/).
