---
title: Security questions with random answers
date: 2026-11-02
summary: Logins can record security questions in their recovery info, and the generator makes random answers.
tags: [core, mobile]
---

Recovery info now holds security questions. Answers are hidden like passwords, and **Random answer** makes one from five random words, which is much safer than a true answer. Keeper and Enpass imports fill them in. See [Recovery info](/docs/features/recovery-info/).
