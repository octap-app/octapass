date: 2026-10-24

tags: [mobile, security]
---

The iPhone app is taking shape in `apps/mobile` (Tauri 2). It shows the web vault's screens, but the keys stay in the app's native process, which links the Rust core and syncs with your server itself.

- Sign up, sign in, lock and unlock, every item type, sync and the trash, offline unlock from the encrypted store on the phone.
- Unlock with Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode. The secret that unlocks the vault is in the Keychain behind the device lock, and a new face or fingerprint invalidates it.
- An AutoFill extension for passwords, passkeys (iOS 17) and one-time codes (iOS 18). It reads the same encrypted store as the app through an App Group, asks for Face ID or the passcode first, and never goes online.
- Wi-Fi Connect for Wi-Fi networks, and Show as QR code and a QR scanner, all on the phone.

It's a preview: it has only run in the Simulator, and there are no signed builds yet. See [Mobile apps](/docs/platforms/mobile/).
