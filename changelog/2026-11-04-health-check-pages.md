---
title: Each Health check opens its own list
date: 2026-11-04
summary: Health now shows your score and a tile per check; choosing one opens that check's passwords on a page of their own instead of one long page with every list.
tags: [web, desktop, extension, mobile]
---

Health used to show the score, a row of counts, and then every check's list underneath, all on one page. With a few dozen warnings that was a long scroll to find the one list you wanted.

Now Health is an overview: your score, any alerts, and a tile for each check with how many items it found. Choose **Weak passwords**, **Reused passwords**, **Found in data breaches** or any other tile and that check's items open on their own page, where **Change password** and **Dismiss** work as before. **Health** at the top of the page takes you back. Dismissed warnings and the email breach alerts switch have their own pages too, and the recovery map is a link at the bottom of Health.

This is in the web vault, the desktop app, the extension's tab and the phone apps: on iPhone and Android each check is a row on Health that opens its list. On the Mac app, which shares the iPhone app's screens, it's the same. See [security dashboard](/docs/features/security-dashboard/).
