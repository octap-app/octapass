## What's new

- **Device unlock is offered right away.** After you sign up or sign in on the iPhone, Android or desktop app, Octa Pass asks "Use Face ID to unlock Octa Pass?" (or Touch ID, your fingerprint, face or screen lock, Windows Hello, or your passcode). **Turn on** runs your device's check once, and from then on the app and AutoFill ask for it instead of your master password. Choose **Not now** and Octa Pass asks once more the next time you unlock with your master password, then never again. You still enter your master password every 14 days. See [biometric unlock](/docs/features/biometric-unlock/).
- **The iPhone's AutoFill sheet says where to turn it on** when device unlock is off.

## Fixed

- **Arabic in the iPhone's AutoFill sheet and camera prompt.** The Arabic translations weren't included in the app, so these showed in English.
