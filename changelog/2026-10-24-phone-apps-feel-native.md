The iPhone and Android apps show the same screens as the web vault, and until now they sometimes behaved like a web page in a browser. They now behave like the other apps on your phone. See [the mobile apps](/docs/platforms/mobile/#on-both-phones).

## What's new

- **No more zooming.** Pinching or double-tapping no longer zooms the screen, and tapping into a field no longer zooms into it. Text size follows your phone's setting (Dynamic Type on iPhone, Font size on Android) instead.
- **Screens slide** in and out as you go into an item or a Settings page and back, or fade with Reduce Motion on. On iPhone, swipe from the edge to go back.
- **Menus and dialogs open as sheets** from the bottom of the screen.
- **The keyboard** no longer covers the field you're typing in, and the tab bar hides while it's open.
- **Haptics** when you copy, save or delete.
- **Exports open the share sheet**, so they can be saved to Files or sent to another app. Before, an export from the phone went nowhere.
- **The status bar** matches the app's theme, light or dark.
- **Touch feel:** no grey flash on taps, no page bounce, no long-press menus on buttons and lists, and buttons react to your finger rather than staying highlighted after a tap. Values and notes can still be selected and copied.

The web vault on a phone's browser gets some of this too: fields are at least 16px on touch screens, so Safari no longer zooms into them, and menus and dialogs are sheets on narrow screens.
