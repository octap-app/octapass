---
title: Move your passwords to the Mac app straight from Apple Passwords
date: 2026-11-04
summary: On macOS 26, the Passwords app's Export Data to Another App can now send your passwords, passkeys and codes to the Octa Pass desktop app, with no file.
tags: [desktop, docs]
---

On macOS 26, password managers can hand their items to each other directly, with no file in between. In the Passwords app, choose **File → Export Data to Another App**, then **Octa Pass**. Before, the Mac listed no app to receive them; only the iPhone app could.

Octa Pass comes forward, asks you to unlock if it's locked, and shows how many items arrived. Nothing is saved until you choose **Import**, and the items go into your Personal vault. Cancel, or lock Octa Pass, and they're forgotten. The items are read inside the app, never in its window.

See [moving from another password manager on the Mac](/docs/platforms/desktop/#moving-from-another-password-manager-on-the-mac). The new messages are also in Arabic.
