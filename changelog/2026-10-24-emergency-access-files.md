---
title: Emergency contacts can open your files
date: 2026-10-24
summary: Once emergency access is released, your contact can download the files attached to the items they can see, decrypted on their device.
tags: [server, security, website]
---

Emergency access now covers attachments. When your trusted contact opens your vault after the waiting period, items with files show a **Files** list, and each file downloads and decrypts on their device, the same way your own files do.

They only get files from what you chose to share with them: everything, or the one vault you picked. If you deny the request or remove the contact, downloads stop straight away. The server still only ever sees encrypted files. See [emergency access](/docs/features/emergency-access/).

For developers: the new endpoint is in the [sync API](/docs/developers/sync-api/#emergency-access).
