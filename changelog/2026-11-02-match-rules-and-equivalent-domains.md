---
title: Website match rules and your own equivalent domains
date: 2026-11-02
summary: Choose how each website on a login matches, and tell Octa Pass which sites share one sign-in.
tags: [core, extension, desktop, mobile, security]
---

Each website on a login now has a **Fill on** rule: this site and its subdomains, this exact host, pages under this address, a regular expression, or never. The rule applies when suggesting and again when filling.

**Settings → Equivalent domains** adds your own groups of sites that share one sign-in and turns built-in groups off. Public suffixes such as `co.uk` are refused. The list is encrypted in your vault and follows your account. See [Autofill](/docs/features/autofill/#domain-matching).
