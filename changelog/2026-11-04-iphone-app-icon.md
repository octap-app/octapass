---
title: The iPhone app has the Octa Pass icon
date: 2026-11-04
summary: The iPhone and iPad app showed the icon of the framework the first phone app was built with. It now has the Octa Pass icon, on the home screen and in App Store Connect.
tags: [mobile]
---

The iPhone and iPad app still carried a placeholder icon from the framework the first phone app was built with (Tauri). It now shows the Octa Pass icon: the white monogram on the blue-to-green gradient, as on the Mac, Android, the browser extension and the website.

The icon comes from the same source as the other apps' icons (`brand/logo-tile.svg`), so a change to the logo now reaches every Apple app at once.
