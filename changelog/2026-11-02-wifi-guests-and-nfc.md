---
title: Share Wi-Fi for a day, or write it to an NFC tag
date: 2026-11-02
summary: Give a guest a one-time link as a QR code, or write a network to an NFC tag on Android.
tags: [mobile, desktop]
---

**Share for a day** on a Wi-Fi network makes a one-time link that opens once within 24 hours, shown as a QR code. Your item stays yours, and you can cancel the link under Sharing.

On Android, **Write to NFC tag** puts a network on a tag so guests with an Android phone tap and confirm to join. A tag isn't encrypted, so the app says that first. iPhones don't join from tags, and WEP, WPA3-only and Enterprise networks can't go on one. See [Wi-Fi networks](/docs/features/wifi/).
