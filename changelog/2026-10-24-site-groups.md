---
title: One row for several accounts on the same website
date: 2026-10-24
summary: When you have several logins for the same website, the item list shows one row for it with the number of accounts. Click or tap it to see the logins under it.
tags: [web, extension, desktop, mobile]
---

After importing hundreds of logins, a site you have three accounts on took three rows. Now the list shows one row for it, with the website's address and a count such as **3 accounts**. Click or tap the row and the logins open right under it, indented; click or tap again to close it.

- It's the exact website: `a.example.com` and `b.example.com` stay apart, while `https://www.a.example.com` and `a.example.com` count as one.
- The row sits in the current section and follows the list's sort order. A website with one login stays a normal row.
- Turn it off with **Combine accounts for the same website** in the **Sort and group** menu.
- Search results, the extension's **This site** view and autofill menus still list each login on its own.

It works the same in the web vault, the desktop and phone apps and the browser extension, in English and Arabic. See [sorting and grouping](/docs/features/sorting-and-grouping/).
