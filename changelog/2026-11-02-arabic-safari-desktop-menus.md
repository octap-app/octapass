---
title: Arabic in the Safari app and the desktop menu bar
date: 2026-11-02
summary: The screen that shows where to turn on the Safari extension, and the desktop app's standard Edit and Window menus, now come in Arabic.
tags: [extension, desktop]
---

The two places still in English for Arabic readers are done. The Octa Pass app that carries the Safari extension follows your system language, right to left in Arabic. The desktop app's whole menu bar follows the language of the vault window, including the standard File, Edit, View, Window and Help menus.
