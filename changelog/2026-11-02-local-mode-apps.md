---
title: Use Octa Pass on this device only, on your computer and phone
date: 2026-11-02
summary: Vaults with no account come to the desktop app and the iPhone and Android apps, with encrypted backups, automatic folder backups, several vaults on one device and Turn on sync.
tags: [desktop, mobile, security]
---

The desktop app and the iPhone and Android apps can now keep a vault with no account and no server. You can keep several on one device. Each vault has its own master password and, where the computer has a security chip the app can use, is bound to it, so a copy of its file is useless elsewhere.

Back up to an encrypted `.octapass-backup` file, or pick a folder and each change is backed up there, keeping the newest seven. **Turn on sync** moves a vault into a new account as it is, encrypted.

Auto-type and AutoFill now follow the **Ask on every fill** switch. See [local vaults](/docs/features/local-vault/).
