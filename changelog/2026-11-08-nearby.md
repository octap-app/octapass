---
title: Nearby lists what belongs to the place you're at
date: 2026-11-08
summary: Save places like home or the office, add items to them, and Nearby lists the place you're at first, worked out on your device from its Wi-Fi network or location, with nothing sent anywhere.
tags: [decision, core, extension, desktop, mobile, security]
---

**Nearby**, in the sidebar under Recently created, isn't "Soon" any more. It lists your places with the items that belong to each: the Wi-Fi password and the alarm code at home, the door code and the printer's login at the office. The place you're at comes first, as **Now nearby: Office**, with its items grouped by type; your other places follow, nearest first when your device knows its location, each with a rough distance.

- **Places** have a name, the Wi-Fi networks that mean you're there, and optionally a point and a radius (150 m unless you choose). Use your current location or type the coordinates. Octa Pass never looks up an address or loads a map.
- **Add to a place** on any item, or make a new item from a place in Nearby. When you save a new item while you're at one of your places, Octa Pass offers to add it there. You can turn that off in Settings.
- **Your device works out where it is**, from the Wi-Fi network it's on and, if you allow it, one location reading while the app is open. No background location, no geofencing, no location history.
- **Places are encrypted items** in your vault. The server can't see their names, locations, networks, or which items belong to them, and it never learns where you are. A place in a shared vault is seen by the vault's members, and Octa Pass warns before you save one with a location there.
- Places are in Octa Pass exports and come back when you import one.

The browser extension shows Nearby without your location, because extensions can't see Wi-Fi and asking for location would add a permission for everyone. The Mac app knows where it is by its location only for now: it doesn't read the Wi-Fi network yet. See [Nearby](/docs/features/nearby/) and [what the server sees](/docs/security/what-the-server-sees/). The decisions are in `plans/01-product-spec.md` and `plans/03-security-model.md`.
